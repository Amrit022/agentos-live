How the strategy works

Forex Master Pro v4.0 uses a 7-indicator confluence engine — it only opens trades when the EMA Ribbon (9/21/50/200), RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, Stochastic Oscillator, Volume, and Candle Patterns (Engulfing/Pin Bar) reach the minimum confluence score on the same candle. An ATR Volatility Regime Filter additionally skips dead or chaotic markets. This filters low-quality setups before they cost you money.

Risk is managed automatically with percentage-based lot sizing, an auto-resetting drawdown circuit breaker, partial take profit (50% at 1R, rest to 3R), smart recovery lot reduction after consecutive losses, time-based exits for stagnant trades, and break-even plus ATR trailing stops.
