What Is a Confluence Expert Advisor for MT5?

Most forex robots fire trades on a single indicator signal. That creates noise. A confluence EA waits until multiple independent indicators agree — reducing false entries and improving trade quality.

How Forex Master Pro uses confluence

Forex Master Pro EA v4.0 only opens a trade when indicators align to meet the confluence score threshold on the same candle:

  • EMA Ribbon (9, 21, 50, 200) — trend direction confirmed
  • RSI — momentum not overextended
  • MACD — trend strength and crossover confirmation
  • Bollinger Bands — price at a logical pullback zone
  • Stochastic Oscillator — momentum shifts and oversold/overbought reversals confirmed
  • Volume — above-average volume confirms institutional participation
  • Candle Pattern Recognition — Engulfing/Pin Bar continuation patterns

The result: fewer trades, higher-quality setups, and less exposure to choppy ranging markets.
