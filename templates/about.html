About NEXORA

Our Mission

At NEXORA, we believe that high-quality, futuristic gear and lifestyle essentials shouldn't carry astronomical markups. Traditionally, products go from factories to wholesalers, then to distributors, then to retail stores, piling up warehouse rents and transportation fees at every stage. You, the customer, end up footing the bill.

NEXORA operates differently. By leveraging next-generation global dropshipping logistics, we completely eliminate the middleman. We connect directly with trusted suppliers, source products straight from manufacturing floors, and ship them straight to your door. This lean, zero-waste operation allows us to provide premium products at fractions of retail costs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is dropshipping and how does it work?

Dropshipping is a retail fulfillment model where a store doesn't keep the products it sells in stock. Instead, when you buy a product, we purchase the item from our global supplier and they ship it directly to your address. This allows us to offer thousands of items without warehouse overhead.

How long does shipping take?

Standard global shipping takes between 2 to 7 business days depending on your location and the product's category. Each product listing displays an estimated delivery timeline. You will receive a tracking link via email as soon as the package leaves the manufacturer.

Are there any customs duties or import taxes?

For most major destinations, customs fees are pre-cleared or included in the product's price. If any local import duties are assessed on delivery, please contact our support desk with a copy of the receipt and we will reimburse you.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 100% Satisfaction Guarantee. If you are not satisfied with your purchase, you can contact us at support@agentosacademy.com within 30 days of receiving the item to arrange a return and request a full refund.
