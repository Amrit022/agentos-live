📋 CoR for Supervisors & Schedulers

Advanced Chain of Responsibility training covering legal duties, scheduling fatigue management, compliant rostering, and operational due diligence documentation.

⏱️ Duration: 12 Hours 📖 8 Modules & Quizzes 🎓 Certificate of Completion
Module 01

Scheduler Duties & Legal Foundations

Identify your legal obligations as a scheduler or supervisor under the HVNL. Learn how scheduling directly impacts transport safety and can trigger Chain of Responsibility duties.

Module 02

Fatigue Risk Management for Schedulers

Understand the sleep science behind driver fatigue. Learn how to identify risk indicators and implement scheduling safety margins to prevent driver exhaustion.

Module 03

Creating Compliant Rosters & Trip Plans

Drafting realistic schedules that incorporate mandatory rest breaks, account for traffic variations, and avoid pushing drivers past legal work/rest limits.

Module 04

Monitoring Driver Work & Rest Hours

Practical systems for auditing driver logbooks, verifying Electronic Work Diaries (EWDs), and handling cases where scheduling conflicts require route modifications.

Module 05

Documenting Operational Due Diligence

Learn what records you must keep to prove you took all "reasonably practicable" steps to ensure transport safety. Keep your schedule changes audit-ready.

Module 06

Speed Management & Consignee Obligations

Managing speed limits across schedules. Addressing receiver loading dock delays that pressure drivers to speed, and implementing dock reservation safety buffers.

Module 07

Subcontractor and Supplier Compliance

How to vet and monitor third-party carriers to ensure they adhere to your organization's fatigue, mass, and safety standards under CoR duties.

Module 08

Operational Safety Assurance & Auditing

Conducting safety audits on your scheduling procedures. Ensuring your systems adapt to the 2026 HVNL regulatory frameworks and remain fully compliant.
