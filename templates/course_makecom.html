📦 Load Restraint & Mass Management

Master performance-based load restraint standards, mass limits (GML, CML, HML), dimension requirements, and the 2026 changes to general access limits.

Performance Standards: 0.8g forward · 0.5g sideways/rearward · 0.2g vertical

⏱️ Duration: 10 Hours 📖 8 Modules & Quizzes 🎓 Certificate of Completion
Module 01

Introduction to Load Restraint

Why load restraint matters, the physics of cargo movement during braking, turning, and road undulations, and the legal framework governing load security in Australia.

Module 02

Performance-Based Standards

Understanding the g-force requirements: 0.8g forward (emergency braking), 0.5g sideways and rearward (cornering/reversing), and 0.2g vertically upward (road surface variations).

Module 03

Methods of Restraint

Lashing (tie-downs), blocking, containing, friction, and combination methods. When to use each method and how to calculate the minimum number of lashings required.

Module 04

Pre-Departure Load Inspections

Conducting thorough pre-departure inspections, checking lashing tension, identifying load shift risks, and documenting your inspection for compliance evidence.

Module 05

Mass Limits: GML, CML & HML

Understanding General Mass Limits, Concessional Mass Limits, and Higher Mass Limits. Axle group configurations, bridge formula calculations, and the 2026 GML increases.

Module 06

Dimension & Configuration Limits

Vehicle length limits (new 20m general access under 2026 reforms), width, height, and overhang restrictions. Restricted access vehicles and permit requirements.

Module 07

Practical Load Scenarios

Real-world case studies: restraining steel coils, palletised goods, loose bulk cargo, machinery, and dangerous goods. Common failures and how to prevent them.

Module 08

CoR Obligations for Loaders & Consignors

How Chain of Responsibility applies to loading practices. Consignor and loader duties, shared responsibility with drivers, and documentation requirements.
