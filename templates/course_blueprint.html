🛡️ Chain of Responsibility Awareness

Understand your legal obligations under Australia's Heavy Vehicle National Law (HVNL). Essential training for every party in the transport supply chain.

Module 01

Introduction to Chain of Responsibility

What CoR means, its history in Australian transport law, and why every party in the supply chain has a legal duty of care for heavy vehicle safety.

Module 02

The Primary Safety Duty — Section 26C

Deep dive into the primary safety duty under Section 26C of the HVNL. What "so far as is reasonably practicable" means in practice and how it applies to your role.

Module 03

Who Is in the Chain?

Identify every party in the chain: employers, drivers, schedulers, loaders, consignors, consignees, loading managers, prime contractors, and executive officers.

Module 04

The 5 Key Compliance Areas

Overview of speed, fatigue, mass/dimension/loading, load restraint, and vehicle standards — the five pillars of heavy vehicle compliance that form the backbone of CoR.

Module 05

Speed & Fatigue Obligations

How CoR applies to speed management and fatigue. Understanding work/rest limits, scheduling pressures, and the business practices that contribute to non-compliance.

Module 06

Mass, Loading & Restraint Obligations

CoR duties related to mass limits, correct load distribution, and ensuring all cargo is restrained to performance-based standards before departure.

Module 07

Penalties, Enforcement & Case Studies

Real-world prosecution case studies, financial penalties for individuals and businesses, and how regulators investigate and enforce CoR breaches.

Module 08

HVNL 2026 Reforms & Your Obligations

How the 2026 reforms strengthen CoR duties, the new "Unfit to Drive" expanded obligations, and what changes from 1 August 2026 with no grace period.
