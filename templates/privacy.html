Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At SafeFleet Academy (operated via SafeFleetacademy.com), we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website, purchase compliance training courses, or access our Training Portal.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the data absolutely necessary to provide you with our services:

  • Account Information: When you purchase a training module, we collect your name and email address to provision your Training Portal account.
  • Payment Data: All payments are securely processed via PayPal. We do not store or process your credit card or bank details directly on our servers.
  • Usage Analytics: We may collect anonymised usage data (such as page views, course progress, and interaction metrics) to improve the layout and functionality of our platform.

2. Training Content & Educational Disclaimer

Our compliance training courses cover topics including Chain of Responsibility (CoR), Fatigue Management, Mass/Dimension/Loading, and related heavy vehicle regulations. Please note:

  • All training content is provided for educational awareness purposes only and does not constitute legal advice.
  • SafeFleet Academy does not collect or store any operational data about your fleet, vehicles, or drivers through our training platform.
  • Any assessments or quizzes completed within our modules are used solely to track your learning progress and are not shared with regulators or third parties.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for the following operational purposes:

  • Delivering training modules and granting Training Portal access.
  • Tracking your course progress and completion status.
  • Sending important transactional emails, system updates, and course-related notifications.
  • Sending our Compliance Updates newsletter (only if you explicitly opted in).

4. Data Security

All passwords stored in our database are cryptographically hashed using standard security protocols. We enforce HTTPS encryption across all pages to ensure your data is secure in transit. We are committed to handling your personal information in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

5. Your Rights

You have the right to request an export of the data we hold on you, or request full account deletion at any time by contacting us at support@SafeFleetacademy.com.
