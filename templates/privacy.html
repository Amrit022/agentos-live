Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

At AgentOS, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website, digital products, and AI automation training materials.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect the data absolutely necessary to provide you with our services:

  • Account Information: When you purchase a product, we collect your name and email address to provision your Customer Portal.
  • Payment Data: All payments are securely processed via Stripe. We do not store or process your credit card numbers directly on our servers.
  • Usage Analytics: We may collect anonymized telemetry data (such as page views and interaction metrics) to improve the layout and functionality of our platform.

2. AI API & Third-Party Integrations

Because our training involves building AI automations (using tools like OpenAI, Voiceflow, and Make.com), it is important to understand how data flows in these systems:

  • We do not have access to the API keys you generate for third-party LLMs (e.g., OpenAI, Anthropic) when following our blueprints.
  • Any conversational data processed by the chatbots you build remains strictly between you, your clients, and your chosen AI providers. AgentOS does not monitor or store your bot transcripts.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for the following operational purposes:

  • Delivering digital products and granting portal access.
  • Sending important transactional emails and system updates.
  • Sending our AI Strategy Newsletter (only if you explicitly opted in).

4. Data Security

All passwords stored in our database are cryptographically hashed using standard security protocols. We enforce HTTPS encryption across all pages to ensure your data is secure in transit.

You have the right to request an export of the data we hold on you, or request full account deletion at any time by contacting us at amritgupta0412@gmail.com.
