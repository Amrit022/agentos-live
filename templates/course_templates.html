⏱️ Fatigue Management Training

Comprehensive training on Standard Hours, Basic Fatigue Management (BFM), work diaries, Electronic Work Diaries (EWD), and the new ACA framework under HVNL 2026.

Module 01

Why Fatigue Management Matters

The science of fatigue, its impact on reaction times and decision-making, and why fatigue is one of the leading causes of heavy vehicle incidents in Australia.

Module 02

Standard Hours — Work & Rest Limits

Maximum 12 hours work in 24 hours, minimum 7 continuous hours stationary rest, short break requirements at 5.5h, 8h, and 11h, and the 72-hour weekly cap.

Module 03

Basic Fatigue Management (BFM)

Extended hours under BFM: up to 14 hours in 24 hours, break thresholds at 6.25h, 9h, and 12h, the 144-hour fortnightly cap, and mandatory 24-hour rest after 84 hours of work.

Module 04

Work Diary Compliance

When a work diary is required (100km+ from base), how to correctly fill out daily sheets, recording work time vs rest time, and common mistakes that lead to infringements.

Module 05

Electronic Work Diaries (EWD)

How EWDs work, approved EWD systems, transitioning from paper to electronic, and the compliance advantages of digital record-keeping.

Module 06

Fatigue Risk Management for Schedulers

Scheduler obligations under CoR, creating compliant rosters, avoiding scheduling pressures that force drivers to breach fatigue limits, and documentation requirements.

Module 07

Fitness for Duty & Unfit to Drive

The expanded "Unfit to Drive" duty under HVNL 2026 — covering illness, injury, mental health, drugs, and alcohol. Shared responsibilities between drivers and employers.

Module 08

ACA Transition & HVNL 2026 Changes

How the new Alternative Compliance Accreditation (ACA) replaces BFM/AFM, what operators need to do to transition, and the timeline for NHVAS phase-out.
