⚖️ CoR Due Diligence for Executives

Executive officer compliance framework: Understand your personal liability, the six elements of due diligence under Section 26D, and how to verify organizational safety systems.

⏱️ Duration: 16 Hours 📖 8 Modules & Quizzes 🎓 Certificate of Completion
Module 01

Executive Officer Liability & HVNL Legal Context

Understanding the severe personal civil and criminal liabilities for executive officers under Section 26D of the Heavy Vehicle National Law.

Module 02

The Six Elements of Due Diligence (Section 26D)

An in-depth analysis of the 6 components: Acquire knowledge, Understand operations, Resource safety, Monitor incidents, Ensure compliance, and Verify safety systems.

Module 03

Executive Safety Governance & Reporting Channels

Structuring safety committees, establishing direct safety reporting lines to the board, and designing executive safety dashboard metrics.

Module 04

Legal Defences & Reasonably Practicable Standards

What "reasonably practicable" means for executives, establishing safety assurance documentation, and building a strong legal defense framework.

Module 05

Safe Systems of Work & Operational Risk Registers

Ensuring executive-level oversight of transport risk assessments, hazard logs, and the implementation of hierarchy of controls.

Module 06

Implementing Safety Assurance & Verification Systems

Methods for auditing Safety Management Systems (SMS) performance, conducting management reviews, and verifying corrective actions.

Module 07

Managing Regulator Investigations & Enforcement Notices

Vetting legal responsibilities when dealing with NHVR improvement notices, prohibition notices, or formal prosecution processes.

Module 08

Fostering a Resilient Safety & Compliance Culture

Setting safety policies from the top down, promoting hazard self-reporting, and preparing the executive team for the 2026 HVNL reforms.
