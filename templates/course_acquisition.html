🔍 SMS Implementation & Audit Prep

Build and audit Safety Management Systems using the PSOE methodology. Prepare for the new Heavy Vehicle Accreditation (HVA) — General Safety Accreditation (GSA) and Alternative Compliance Accreditation (ACA).

⚠️ Mandatory from 1 August 2026 — All accredited operators must demonstrate a functioning SMS under the new HVA scheme.
Module 01

What Is a Safety Management System (SMS)?

Understanding SMS as a structured framework for managing safety risks. How SMS differs from ad-hoc safety practices and why it's now mandatory under the HVNL 2026 reforms.

Module 02

The 5 Core SMS Outcomes

Deep dive into the five required outcomes: Leadership & Commitment, Risk Management, People, Assurance & Improvement, and Safety Systems. What auditors look for in each area.

Module 03

PSOE Audit Methodology

The four-level PSOE assessment: Present (does the element exist?), Suitable (is it fit for purpose?), Operating (is it being used?), Effective (is it achieving results?). How to self-assess before a formal audit.

Module 04

Building Your Safety Documentation

Creating compliant safety policies, procedures, work instructions, and forms. Templates for risk registers, incident reports, corrective action plans, and management review records.

Module 05

Risk Assessment & Hazard Identification

Systematic approaches to identifying transport hazards, assessing risk likelihood and consequence, implementing hierarchy of controls, and documenting risk treatment plans.

Module 06

Incident Investigation & Root Cause Analysis

How to conduct effective incident investigations, identify root causes (not just symptoms), implement corrective and preventive actions, and report incidents to the regulator.

Module 07

GSA & ACA Accreditation Pathways

The new Two-Tier HVA accreditation: General Safety Accreditation (GSA) as the baseline, and Alternative Compliance Accreditation (ACA) for operators seeking extended fatigue flexibility. Application process and requirements.

Module 08

Transitioning from NHVAS to HVA

What current NHVAS-accredited operators need to do. The 3-year transition timeline, gap analysis between NHVAS and HVA requirements, and practical steps to achieve compliance.
