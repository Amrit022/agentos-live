05 // FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

CoR is the legal framework under the Heavy Vehicle National Law (HVNL) that extends safety obligations beyond the driver to ALL parties who influence or control transport activities — including employers, schedulers, loaders, consignors, consignees, and executive officers.
Our courses are designed as awareness-level compliance training and provide a Certificate of Completion. They are not nationally accredited RTO qualifications. For nationally accredited units (e.g., TLIF0005, TLIF0009), we recommend pairing our training with an accredited RTO assessment.
Key changes include: mandatory Safety Management Systems (SMS), a new Two-Tier HVA accreditation replacing NHVAS, expanded "Unfit to Drive" duties covering illness and mental health, increased mass limits (GML raised to current CML levels), and extended length limits (19m → 20m). There is NO grace period — compliance is required from Day One (1 August 2026).
