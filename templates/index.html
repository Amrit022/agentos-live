03 // Execution

The Agency Blueprint

How to go from zero experience to a $2,000 client in 30 days.

PHASE 01 (DAYS 1-5)

Skill Acquisition

Create free accounts on Voiceflow and Make.com. Spend 5 days learning the interface. You don't need to code; just learn how to drag and drop nodes to create conversational logic flows.

PHASE 02 (DAYS 6-10)

The Core Offer & Demo

Pick one specific niche (e.g., Real Estate). Build a demo bot tailored specifically for them (e.g., an Open House scheduler). This demo link is your ultimate sales tool.

PHASE 03 (DAYS 11-20)

Hyper-Targeted Outreach

Use LinkedIn, cold email, or Instagram DMs to send short Loom video messages to business owners. Don't sell "AI"; show them their custom demo bot in action.

PHASE 04 (DAYS 21-30)

Closing & Fulfillment

Close your first $2,000 deal. Fulfill the service by simply duplicating your template and changing the branding. Pitch a $500/mo retainer for maintenance. Repeat.
