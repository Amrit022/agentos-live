FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about configuring or running Forex Master Pro EA? Here are quick answers to common developer and user inquiries.

Which currency pairs and timeframes are recommended? +
Forex Master Pro EA is fully optimized for EURUSD on the H1 (1-Hour) timeframe. The EA's internal indicators and Multi-Timeframe confluences pull trend data directly from H4 and D1 to filter noise.
Does the EA work on Hedging and Netting accounts? +
Yes! The EA is designed to comply with both Hedging and Netting account rules. The stops decoupling and custom partial-close logic ensure order modifications work cleanly on all brokers, including MetaQuotes-Demo.
What is the minimum deposit required to start? +
The EA has a built-in safety lot size calculator that adjusts volume based on your account size. You can start with as little as $100 on a standard account using micro lots (0.01).
Does the EA require a VPS to run? +
While not strictly required, using a Virtual Private Server (VPS) is highly recommended. It ensures the EA remains connected and executes orders 24/5 with low latency, bypassing local internet or power interruptions.
