Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What's new in version 4.0 Powerhouse Edition?

v4.0 expands the confluence engine from 5 to 7 indicators with a new Stochastic Oscillator and Candle Pattern Recognition (Engulfing and Pin Bars). It also adds an ATR Volatility Regime Filter that skips dead or chaotic markets, upgrades the circuit breaker to auto-reset after a cooldown once equity recovers, and ships a dedicated Gold (XAUUSD) mode with a stricter 5/7 signal score.

Which currency pairs and timeframes work best?

The EA is optimized for M15, M30, and H1 timeframes on EURUSD, GBPUSD, USDCAD, USDCHF, and XAUUSD. It works best on low-spread ECN/RAW accounts — optimized for OctaFX.

What is the minimum deposit required?

You can start with as little as $100. The EA automatically calculates lot sizes based on your account balance and configured risk percentage (e.g. 0.5% per trade).

What's the difference between buying and renting?

Buying ($149) gives you permanent access with 5 MT5 activations. Renting ($30/month or $75/3 months) gives you temporary access for the rental period — great for testing on a live account before committing to a full purchase.

Can I test the EA before buying?

Yes — download the free demo from MQL5 and run it in the MetaTrader 5 Strategy Tester with your own broker data. No credit card required.

How many MT5 accounts can I use?

The full purchase includes 5 MT5 account activations. Demo accounts are always unlimited. You can request a transfer to a new live account at any time.

Does the EA guarantee profits?

No trading system can guarantee profits. Backtested results are historical and not indicative of future performance. The EA focuses on disciplined execution and strict risk management — never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
