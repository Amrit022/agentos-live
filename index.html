FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to help you deploy and configure the EA successfully.

Which currency pairs and timeframes work best?

The EA is optimized for EURUSD and GBPUSD on the M15 chart, but the multi-timeframe confluence engine automatically reads H1, H4, and D1 data regardless of the chart it runs on. Any major pair with tight spreads will work.

Do I need a VPS to run the EA?

A VPS is recommended so the EA can trade 24/7 without your computer staying on. Every license includes free remote VPS setup assistance to get you deployed on a low-latency server.

What is the minimum deposit required?

You can start with as little as $100 on a cent or micro account. For standard accounts we recommend $500+ so the dynamic lot sizing has room to manage risk at 1–2% per trade.

How is the license bound to my account?

After purchase you send us your MT5 account number and receive a license key bound to it. Demo accounts are always unlimited, and you can request a transfer to a new live account at any time.

Can I test the EA before buying?

Yes — a free demo version is available through the MQL5 marketplace where you can run it in the MetaTrader strategy tester with your own broker data before committing to a license.

Does the EA guarantee profits?

No trading system can guarantee profits. Backtested results are historical and not indicative of future performance. The EA's focus is disciplined execution and strict risk management — never risk money you cannot afford to lose.
